Bragg's law

X-rays are reflected by planes of atoms separated by a distance d. The wave from the second plane travels an extra path of 2d sinθ: only when that path is a whole number of wavelengths do the waves add up and a diffraction peak appears.

Intensity versus angle

Try it: increase N and the peaks become sharper. A large crystal (many planes) gives narrow peaks; a nanocrystal (few planes) gives broad peaks. That is the idea behind the Scherrer equation. The starting value d = 2.49 Å is the (211) plane of Mn3O4.
