Urbach energy

Below the gap, absorption does not drop to zero abruptly: it decays exponentially (the Urbach tail). Its width, the Urbach energy EU, measures structural disorder and defect states in the material.

α = α0 exp[(hν − E0) / EU]  ⇒  ln α = (1/EU) hν + cte

A simulated example: Eg = 2.85 eV and an Urbach tail with EU = 80 meV. It is not measured data.

3. Urbach plot

ln F(R) (or ln α) is plotted against hν. The line is fitted in the exponential region just below Eg; its slope is 1/EU. Drag on the plot to choose it yourself. Typical values: 20–50 meV in good crystals, 50–300 meV in nanoparticles and defective films.
