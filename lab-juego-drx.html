What you learn

  • Peak positions depend on the unit cell (the d-spacings); intensities depend on which atoms sit where.
  • High-symmetry, small cells (silver, rock salt) give few, widely spaced peaks; large or low-symmetry cells give many.
  • Polymorphs such as the three ZnO structures have the same formula but different patterns.
