Photon hunter

You are a semiconductor. Tap only the photons with enough energy to lift an electron across the gap (hν ≥ Eg) and let the others pass. The material changes every 15 seconds.

Ready?

Photons fall with their wavelength written on them. Catch (tap) the ones the material absorbs: λ ≤ 1240 / Eg. Tapping a photon without enough energy, or letting an absorbable one escape, costs a life.

What you learn

  • A photon carries E = hc/λ = 1240/λ(nm) eV: shorter wavelength, more energy.
  • A semiconductor only absorbs photons with E ≥ Eg; the rest pass through. That sets its colour: ZnO is white, CdSe is dark red.
  • This is the absorption edge you measure with UV-Vis and analyse with the Tauc plot.
