What you learn

  • Bragg’s law nλ = 2d sinθ links the angle of each peak to the spacing between lattice planes.
  • The detector sits at 2θ from the direct beam, while the sample turns by θ (θ–2θ geometry).
  • Changing the tube changes λ and shifts every peak: Mo Kα (0.71 Å) puts them at much lower angles than Cu Kα (1.54 Å).
