About Me

I am a Master's student in Physics at the University of Puerto Rico – Mayagüez (UPRM). My research focuses on the synthesis and physical characterization of nanostructured materials, especially manganese oxides such as Mn3O4, prepared as nanoparticles and thin films.

I want to understand how synthesis and deposition conditions control the structure, defects and optical properties of these materials, and to develop simple, low-cost and scalable routes to produce them. I collaborate with the GMIN group at the Universidad Nacional de Trujillo (Peru).

Research interests

Nanomaterials Manganese oxides (Mn3O4) Thin films Magnetron sputtering Spin coating Optical properties Defects & photoluminescence Random lasing

Techniques used in my research

Synthesis

  • Coprecipitation at room temperature
  • Control of solvent and precursor concentration

Thin films

  • Magnetron sputtering
  • Spin coating

Characterization

  • X-ray diffraction (XRD)
  • Raman and FTIR spectroscopy
  • UV-Vis diffuse reflectance
  • Photoluminescence (PL)
  • Scanning electron microscopy (SEM)

Data analysis

  • Scherrer analysis: crystallite size, strain, dislocation density
  • Lattice parameters from XRD
  • Kubelka–Munk and Tauc band gap
  • Crystal structure modeling (VESTA)
