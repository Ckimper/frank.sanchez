Band gap with Tauc

Calculate the optical band gap from your UV-Vis spectrum, using absorbance or diffuse reflectance with the Kubelka–Munk function.

(αhν)1/n = B (hν − Eg)

1. Your data

The example is a simulated spectrum with Eg = 2.85 eV (direct transition), similar to Mn3O4; it is not measured data.

3. Tauc plot

Drag on the Tauc plot to choose the linear region of the edge yourself; the line is extrapolated to the axis and the intercept gives Eg. Use diffuse reflectance for powders (as in my paper) and absorbance for films or solutions. A poorly subtracted baseline or an Urbach tail can shift Eg by a few hundredths of an eV.
