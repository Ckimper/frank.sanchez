Unit converter

Each technique uses its own unit: nm in UV-Vis and PL, eV for the band gap, cm−1 in Raman and FTIR. They all measure the same thing: the energy of a photon.

E (eV) = 1239,84 / λ (nm)  ·  1 eV = 8065,5 cm−1

Raman shift

The Raman spectrometer measures light scattered at another wavelength; the shift in cm−1 is the energy of the vibration. Starting value: the A1g mode of Mn3O4 (658 cm−1) with a 532 nm laser.
